Pre-poll centres are diamonds. A pre-poll centre sits at one address but takes votes from a whole federal division, so its totals describe that division and not the town it is in. Use Election day to read the map geographically.

Ordinary votes only. Postal, absent and other declaration votes are not tied to a booth, so they are not counted here. Booth totals sit below division totals for that reason.

Who counts as independent. Every candidate listed as IND on the ballot. In 2022 that includes Rob Priestly in Nicholls, who did not stand in 2025. Boundaries are the Victorian Electoral Commission districts in force for the November 2026 state election. Street map by CARTO from OpenStreetMap data, when a basemap key is set.
