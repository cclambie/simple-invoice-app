## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Unlimited invoices, free. No trial that runs out, no invoice limit, no account to create.

Most invoice apps lock you out after a handful of invoices, or charge a big subscription just to
send a PDF. This one doesn't. Create as many invoices as you need, for free, supported by ads,
with simple, affordable options if you'd rather not see them.

**How free works**
• Unlimited invoices, always
• Your first 3 invoices each month: just a small banner ad, no pop-ups
• After that, the occasional pop-up ad when you send an invoice
• Remove pop-up ads for $1/month, or all ads for $2/month

**Professional invoices in seconds**
• Clean, standard A4 PDF invoices with your business details and tax number (GST, VAT…)
• Send by email, WhatsApp or any app with the Android share button, or save the PDF
• Invoice numbers continue automatically
• Line items, quantities, tax and totals calculated for you
• Save items you bill often and add them with one tap
• Your bank details printed on each invoice: BSB, sort code, routing number or IBAN, plus
  PayPal, Wise or Revolut for international clients

**Works in more than one currency**
• Bill each client in their own currency (A$, £, US$, €, and more)
• Reports total each currency separately

**Keep track of who's paid**
• Mark invoices paid or unpaid
• Reports for any period: invoiced, paid and outstanding
• Export to CSV for your accountant (Premium)

**Your data stays yours**
• Everything is stored on your phone; no account, no cloud lock-in
• Back up to any folder, including ones synced with Nextcloud, Google Drive, Syncthing or
  your own server (automatic backups with Premium Plus)
• Import clients from your contacts

**Plans**
• Free: unlimited invoices, ad supported
• Premium, $1/month: no pop-up ads, CSV export of one month at a time, manual backup & restore
• Premium Plus, $2/month: no ads at all, CSV export for any period, automatic backups
